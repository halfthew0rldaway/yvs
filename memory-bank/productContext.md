# Product Context: Yangversa Computer (YVS)

## Why This Project Exists
Computer and electronics repair workshops deal with high operational friction:
- Disorganized intake leading to customer disputes over pre-existing device scratches or missing chargers.
- Opaque repair progress leading to repeated customer phone inquiries.
- Unmonitored spare parts inventory resulting in unexpected stockouts or untracked loss.
- Inaccurate staff attendance and lack of physical verification for workshop technicians.
- Missed opportunities for recurring customer maintenance revenue after warranty expiration.

YVS solves these challenges in a unified, cloud-backed ERP accessible across desktop workstations, tablets, and technicians' mobile devices.

## User Roles & Experience Goals

1. **Admin / Frontman**:
   - Rapid customer intake (< 2 minutes) with digital accessories checklist, physical defect mapper, and immediate BPBS printout.
   - Real-time visibility into open tickets, technician workloads, and parts availability.
   - Instant invoice creation with digital signatures and thermal/A4 printing.

2. **Technician**:
   - Mobile-first interface for inspecting tickets, uploading technical diagnostic photos, updating status, and picking parts.
   - Comprehensive QC checklist before releasing completed units.
   - Access to internal Repair Wiki for troubleshooting complex board-level issues.

3. **Owner / Pimpinan**:
   - Executive dashboard tracking revenue, SLA compliance, ticket turnaround time, and technician efficiency.
   - Audit trail of all database operations, sensitive edits, and user logins.
   - Geofenced attendance summaries and leave request approvals.

4. **Customer**:
   - Frictionless self-service ticket status tracking via web portal.
   - Clear WhatsApp notifications and timely maintenance reminders for laptop cleaning and thermal service.
